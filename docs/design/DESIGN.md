## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system projects commanding discretion, fiduciary rigor, and architectural precision. Designed for commercial real estate CEOs, private equity partners, and institutional asset managers, the interface feels quiet, measured, and resolute—delivering "executive calm" in high-stakes environments. It prioritizes clarity over visual noise, replacing neon dashboard tropes with bespoke architectural finishes, disciplined typographic cadence, and restrained warmth.

### Design Movement: Modern Architectural Editorial
The design language combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Modern Architecture**:
- **Monolithic Slate Surfaces**: Deep structural backgrounds paired with paper-crisp warm off-white cards and subtle micro-borders.
- **Warm Fiduciary Accents**: Bespoke terracotta/amber and structured iris anchors that prevent cold enterprise fatigue while preserving institutional dignity.
- **Utilitarian Elegance**: Financial telemetry presented with razor-sharp tabular data and generous negative space reminiscent of an architectural portfolio or private placement memorandum.

## Colors

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`)**: Grounding charcoal-black baseline, providing infinite contrast for financial figures.
- **Surface Tier 1 / Card (`#131B2E`)**: Deep architectural slate for core dashboard containers and financial modules.
- **Surface Tier 2 / Elevated (`#1E293B`)**: Raised modules, drawer layers, and tactile bottom sheets.
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)**: Hairline dividers defining module architecture without heavy grid lines.

### Sector & Category Mapping
- **F&B Category Anchor (`#D97706` / Dark Tone `#B45309`)**: Tailored warm amber/terracotta for hospitality, dining, and activation revenue streams.
- **Non-F&B Asset Anchor (`#6366F1` / Dark Tone `#4338CA`)**: Crisp modern iris/indigo for commercial leases, retail, office suites, and long-term covenants.

### Semantic Performance Tiers (Color-Blind Optimized)
- **Outperforming (`> 1.15x`)**: Deep Forest Emerald (`#10B981` on dark; text paired with accessible `#34D399`). Accompanied by distinct directional chevron `▲`.
- **On Track (`0.85x – 1.15x`)**: Balanced Mineral Slate (`#94A3B8`). Accompanied by neutral level marker `■`.
- **Underperforming (`< 0.85x`)**: Refined Crimson/Rose (`#F43F5E`). Accompanied by directional drop indicator `▼`. Never raw primary red.
- **No Baseline / Inactive**: Neutral Ash (`#64748B`). Accompanied by em-dash `—`.

All text pairings pass WCAG AA standards (>4.5:1 for body and >3:1 for large graphical indicators).

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Tabular Discretion
- **Headlines (Hanken Grotesk)**: Chosen for its sharp geometric terminals, low quirkiness, and balanced editorial stature. Renders executive reporting titles with crisp authority.
- **Body & Controls (Manrope)**: Delivers humanist warmth, open counter spaces, and effortless legibility in dense real estate underwriting notes.
- **Metrics & Currency (JetBrains Mono)**: Strictly enforced for portfolio metrics, Net Operating Income (NOI), cap rates, and financial tables. `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1` guarantees character-column alignment across dynamic variance columns.

Letter spacing is tightened subtly on prominent headlines to maintain architectural density and widened slightly on uppercase category labels for immediate visual scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column fluid grid locked within a maximum 1600px width. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) with outer canvas margins of `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters, reflowing multi-tier revenue feeds into stacked 4-column pairing cards.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column structure with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) edge gutters to maximize telemetry real estate.

### Spacing Rhythms & Interaction Boundaries
- Internal card padding preserves a disciplined `space-lg` (24px) desktop and `space-md` (16px) mobile balance.
- All interactive controls (pills, menu selectors, table rows) conform to a strict minimum physical touch target of 44x44px, using invisible hit slops when compact visual styling (such as 32px height pills) is required.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stratification & Razor Borders
To reflect architectural discipline, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of tonal gradation and precise low-contrast outlines:

1. **Base Layer (Elevation 0 - `#0B0F19`)**: Ground plane, canvas, and main dashboard scaffolding.
2. **Card Surface (Elevation 1 - `#131B2E`)**: Primary metrics, graphs, and performance breakdowns. Bound with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating Modules & Dropdowns (Elevation 2 - `#1E293B`)**: Bound with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` hairline borders and an ambient diffuse shadow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
4. **Tactile Bottom Sheet & Modal Layers (Elevation 3 - `#1E293B`)**: Elevated with a subtle top hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`) and anchored over a 40% blackened backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

### Restrained Curvature
Geometric authority requires tight, intentional corners:
- **Base Components & Metric Cards**: `rounded` (0.25rem / 4px) to `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px). Avoids ballooned radiuses, echoing corporate architectural structures.
- **Interactive Segmented Controls & Pills**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) strictly reserved for period toggles (`Daily`, `Monthly`, `Annual`) and discrete category tags to indicate togglability and touch affordance.
- **Tactile Bottom Sheets**: Top corners sculpted at 16px radius, evoking precision-milled physical paper portfolios.

## Components

### 1. Pill Period Toggles (Daily / Monthly / Annual)
- **Container**: Compact pill container (`height: 38px`, hit-slop expanded to 44px) surfaced in `#0F172A` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active State**: Inset pill with `#1E293B` background, crisp warm white typography (`#F8FAFC`), and an ambient 1px highlight border.
- **Inactive State**: Slate text (`#64748B`), smooth 150ms opacity transition on hover/tap.

### 2. Metric Cards with Micro-Sparklines
- **Frame**: Deep slate surface (`#131B2E`) padded with `1.25rem` (20px).
- **Header**: Micro uppercase label (`label-uppercase`) paired with asset category dot indicator (`#D97706` for F&B, `#6366F1` for Non-F&B).
- **Metric Core**: Big tabular value (`metric-display`) flanked by the semantic pill chip (`+14.2% ▲` in `#10B981`).
- **Integrated Sparkline**: 40px height baseline-free SVG sparkline with clean single-stroke (1.5px) vector path. Amber or Iris gradient drop fill ending at 0% opacity.

### 3. Tactile Bottom Sheet (Mobile First)
- **Handle**: Subtle recessed pill drag indicator (36px width, 4px height, `#475569`, 8px margin from top).
- **Transitions**: Native spring physics translation; auto-expands from 50vh summary to 90vh full tenancy ledger.
- **Header**: Fixed title zone with property valuation and instant cross-asset KPI summary.

### 4. Financial Lists & Data Tables
- **Rows**: Alternating micro-surface rows with `min-height: 48px` to guarantee confident touch selection on mobile.
- **Numerical Cells**: All values aligned right with `JetBrains Mono` tabular lining figures.
- **Status Markers**: Dual indication for color-blind safety—relying on text, geometric symbols (`▲`, `■`, `▼`), and semantic hex accents.

### 5. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action**: Deep warm amber fill (`#D97706`), bold charcoal-slate text (`#0F172A`), zero blur, subtle hover brighten (`#F59E0B`). Minimum dimensions `44px` height.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Outline button with 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border and `#E2E8F0` text.
- **Checkboxes & Form Selectors**: Square with 2px radius; checked state fills with primary amber checkmark and high-contrast dark foreground.
